Live project

Last Round
A night-out companion, with the details together.

Last Round brings venue discovery, a personal drink tracker, group plans, and ride planning into one nightlife interface. Its warm, dark visual direction pairs inviting photography with a dashboard that makes the current night easy to review.

Explore Last Round ↗ (opens a new tab)

Design & development

  • Product design
  • Responsive development
  • Supabase & Stripe
01

A dashboard with a clear starting point

The Tonight screen brings the drink log, water check, personal cap, and evening timeline into a single view. Clear actions let someone start a night or record a drink while the navigation connects discovery, ride planning, and group plans.

02

A warm identity for a practical tool

Warm accents, restrained borders, large type, and bar photography create a nightlife identity without losing the structure of a usable dashboard. Desktop and mobile presentations show how the content adapts to different screens.

03

Language that explains the limits

The live interface describes a personal drink cap as a personal choice and labels BAC estimates with their limitations. Ride planning and responsible-night information give the product's practical details a visible place in the experience.
