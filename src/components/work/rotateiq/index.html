Live project

RotateIQ
Terrain and timing, made easier to explore.

RotateIQ is an interactive Fortnite preparation tool. A map sits at the center of the experience, with controls for position, safe-circle placement, observed enemies, timing, and modeled player movement. The current live tool is at rotateiq.com.

Explore RotateIQ ↗ (opens a new tab)

Design & development

  • Interactive maps
  • Route planning
  • Responsive interface
01

Let the map lead

A large map and adjacent route advice keep the visual planning task close to the settings that influence it. Zoom, pan, placement tools, and playback controls give visitors different ways to inspect a setup.

02

Make the reasoning visible

The interface shows estimated travel time, departure timing, and route reasons. Calibration notes and explanations distinguish preparation estimates from live match information, helping visitors understand what the tool can tell them.

03

Give detailed controls a place

Timing and player settings are grouped by purpose, while additional terrain and precise placement controls can be expanded when needed. The portfolio images below show a source preview; the live tool has evolved since those captures.
