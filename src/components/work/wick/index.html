Wick
An education platform built around practice.

Wick is a source-preview trading education interface with lesson navigation, interactive practice tools, paper trading, and an AI coach interface. The portfolio shows the supplied build; a production site and live coach connection have not been verified.

Design & development

  • Education platform
  • Interactive tools
  • AI coach interface
01

Connect learning and practice

Lesson navigation gives the interface a clear educational structure. Practice and paper-trading tools sit alongside the learning material so the design can support both reading and interaction.

02

Keep a complex subject navigable

The visual presentation uses distinct regions for navigation, content, and supporting tools. This separates the visitor's current task from the wider set of available features.

03

Show the actual supplied build

The desktop and mobile screenshots are evidence of the source-preview interface. They demonstrate its design and layout, without claiming production availability or investment outcomes.
