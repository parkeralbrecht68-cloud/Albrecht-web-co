Website design services / Small business websites

Custom website design for your small business.

A business website should make three things clear: what you do, who you help, and how to take the next step. Albrecht Web Co. builds custom coded websites around those questions, with a visual identity that belongs to your business.

Compare website packages ↗
01

Start with the questions your customers ask.

Your services, business details, examples, and contact information give the design its structure. A service business may need a clear inquiry path; a growing company may need more detailed product and service sections. The project starts by agreeing on that scope so the pages have a purpose before the visual details are added.

02

Make the first impression work on every screen.

Responsive website design adapts the layout to phones, tablets, and desktops. Readable type, comfortable tap targets, and navigation that makes sense on a smaller screen help visitors explore your business. The goal is a coherent experience, with your most useful information easy to reach.

03

Give your content a search-friendly structure.

Descriptive page titles, headings, internal links, and clear URLs help people and search engines understand your website. Core business content lives in page HTML, so visitors can read it without a graphics animation. Expanded on-page and technical SEO setup is included in the Deluxe package; the agreed package and scope define the work.

04

Work directly with the person building your website.

Parker Albrecht is the owner and developer at Albrecht Web Co. You can discuss your ideas, services, and website goals directly with him. Basic and Professional packages have a one-time project price with no monthly plan fee; Deluxe includes ongoing monthly care. Domain, hosting, paid tools, and expanded scope are confirmed separately.
